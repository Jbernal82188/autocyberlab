If you monitor real-time coolant temperatures, check Short and Long Term Fuel Trims (STFT/LTFT), or log boost pressure after an ECU remap using apps like Torque Pro, OBDFusion, or BimmerLink, you have likely wondered: **can I leave my OBD2 Bluetooth scanner plugged in all the time?**

The short answer is **no, you should never leave a generic OBD2 dongle plugged in permanently**. While it will not fry your Engine Control Unit (ECU), keeping a wireless adapter connected 24/7 exposes your vehicle to two major hazards: parasitic battery drain and unauthorized access to your car's internal CAN Bus network.

## 1. The Electrical Issue: Why the OBD2 Port Never Sleeps

Unlike your 12V accessory socket or USB multimedia ports—which cut power when you turn off the ignition—**Pin 16 of the standard SAE J1962 OBD2 connector is wired directly to the vehicle's battery (+12V constant power)**.

### How Much Battery Does an ELM327 Adapter Drain?
* **Cheap ELM327 Clones:** Generic adapters lack a true low-power sleep state. They draw between **40 mA and 120 mA** continuously, draining a standard automotive battery within 4 to 5 days of parking.
* **Smart Adapters (OBDLink MX+ / Vgate vLinker):** High-end transceivers feature automatic sleep modes that drop current draw below **3 mA**, making them electrically safer. However, battery life is only half the story.

## 2. The Cybersecurity Risk: Direct CAN Bus Access

Plugging a wireless dongle into your OBD2 port connects a radio transceiver directly to your vehicle's **Controller Area Network (CAN Bus)**.

### Three Critical Wireless OBD2 Vulnerabilities
* **Default PINs and Unencrypted Connections:** Over 90% of budget Bluetooth OBD2 scanners use hardcoded pairing PINs such as `1234` or `0000` that cannot be changed in the firmware. WiFi-based OBD2 adapters frequently broadcast an open, unencrypted network outside the vehicle while parked.
* **24/7 Signal Broadcasting:** Because Pin 16 powers the adapter overnight, anyone within 20 to 30 feet of your driveway with a laptop or smartphone can discover and pair with a generic dongle.
* **Arbitrary CAN Frame Injection:** Using standard `AT` commands, an attacker can inject hexadecimal packets onto the Body Control Module (BCM) bus to unlock doors or disable factory alarms on vehicles lacking a modern Security Gateway (SGW).

## 3. How to Safely Run Permanent OBD2 Telemetry

1. **Use an OBD2 Extension Cable with a Physical Power Switch:** This costs under $10 and physically cuts the +12V line on Pin 16 with the flip of a switch when you park.
2. **Choose Adapters with Physical Pairing Buttons:** Devices like the *OBDLink MX+* or *OBDLink CX* refuse new Bluetooth connections unless you physically press the button on the unit.
3. **Install a Mechanical OBD2 Port Lock:** Prevent physical key-cloning attacks by securing the connector housing with an aluminum port lock.

## Conclusion

If you use a generic wireless scanner, **unplug it every time you exit the vehicle**. For a permanent digital gauge setup on a tuned car, always pair a smart sleep-mode adapter with a physical power cutoff switch.
